## About

Compass Turns - Aviation is an educational Android app designed to help student pilots practise magnetic compass turns, understand heading corrections, and visualize turning behavior. It provides a streamlined calculation page and an interactive compass simulation.

> **Aviation safety notice:** This application is for education and ground training only. It is not a certified flight instrument, navigation system, or operational flight-planning tool. Calculations and animations are simplified training aids and must not be relied upon to operate an aircraft. Always follow approved training material, applicable procedures, and your instructor's guidance.

## Features

- **Turn calculations:** Set aircraft and target headings to explore magnetic compass turning corrections.
- **Turn direction:** Choose left, shortest, or right turns.
- **Turn summary:** Review the calculated heading and related turn information in a compact layout.
- **Interactive visualization:** Animate a simulated compass turn with start, pause, and reset controls.
- **Regional settings:** Select a training region and adjust supported correction settings.
- **Personalization:** Light, dark, and system appearance modes.
- **Responsive interface:** Designed for Android phones and larger screens.
